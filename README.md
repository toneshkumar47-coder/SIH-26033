Problem Statement: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers' earnings and increase consumer prices.

Project Title: FARM DIRECT – A Smart Farmer-to-Consumer Marketplace

SIH Project Proposal
1. Introduction
Agriculture is one of the most important occupations in India. Farmers work hard to produce vegetables, fruits, grains and other agricultural products.

However, farmers do not always receive a fair price for their products because multiple intermediaries are involved in selling them.

At the same time, consumers pay higher prices for agricultural products.

Our project, FARM DIRECT, aims to connect farmers directly with consumers through an online platform.

2. Problem Identification
The current agricultural marketing system has several problems:

Farmers receive low prices for their products.

Intermediaries take a large share of the profit.

Consumers pay higher prices.

Farmers have limited access to markets.

There is no easy way to compare prices between buyers and sellers.

3. Proposed Solution
We propose a digital platform called FARM DIRECT.

It connects farmers, consumers and retailers directly without unnecessary intermediaries.

The platform provides:

Direct buying and selling.

Live market price information.

Farmer product listings with photos.

Online ordering and payment.

Location-based farmer discovery.

Delivery and order tracking.

Unique feature – Group Harvest Deals: Nearby consumers can combine their orders for products from the same farmer. This helps farmers sell larger quantities in one transaction and reduces delivery costs for consumers.

4. Technical Approach
The system works through the following steps:

Farmer registers using a mobile number.

Farmer uploads product details, quantity, price and photos.

The system displays products to nearby consumers and bulk buyers.

Consumers compare prices and place orders.

The Group Harvest Deals feature combines nearby orders.

AI predicts demand and suggests suitable delivery routes.

Farmer receives payment directly.

Consumers receive their products.

6. Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility:

The application can be developed using affordable technologies.

Farmers can use smartphones to access the platform.

Consumers can place orders from their homes.

The system can initially be tested in one village or town.

Possible challenges:

Lack of internet access in some villages.

Difficulty in using digital applications.

Transportation and delivery costs.

Quality and freshness of products.

Solutions:

Provide a simple Tamil and English interface.

Offer voice-based instructions.

Connect farmers with local delivery partners.

Allow product quality ratings and feedback.

7. Impact and Benefits
Benefits to farmers:

Better selling prices.

Direct access to customers.

Reduced dependence on intermediaries.

Faster payments.

Wider market opportunities.

Benefits to consumers:

Lower purchasing costs.

Fresh agricultural products.

Transparent prices.

Convenient online shopping.

Environmental benefits:

Reduced unnecessary transportation.

Better delivery planning.

Less food wastage through demand prediction.

8. Innovation and Uniqueness
Our project introduces the following features:

Group Harvest Deals: Combines orders from nearby consumers to reduce delivery expenses.

Smart Demand Prediction: AI estimates which products consumers will need.

Fair Price Alert: Notifies farmers when the selling price is unusually low compared with available market information.

Village Collection Point: Farmers can combine their products at a nearby collection centre.

Tamil Voice Assistant: Helps farmers who have difficulty reading or typing.

These features aim to make agricultural trading easier, more transparent and affordable.

9. Expected Outcomes
Increase farmers' income.

Reduce consumer purchasing costs.

Reduce unnecessary middlemen.

Improve agricultural market connections.

Support small-scale farmers.

Create a more efficient supply chain.

10. Conclusion
FARM DIRECT is a proposed digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.

By using technology, AI-based demand prediction and smart delivery management, we aim to reduce unnecessary intermediaries.

Our main goal is simple:

"More Profit for Farmers, Less Price for Consumers, Better Future for Agriculture."
